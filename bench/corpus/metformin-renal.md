# Metformin: renal function (bench fixture)

Synthetic bench fixture written for Driftline's load test. It is not medical guidance.

## Before starting

Measure eGFR before starting metformin. Metformin is contraindicated when eGFR is below
30 mL/min/1.73 m2. Starting metformin is not recommended when eGFR is between 30 and 45.

## During therapy

Check eGFR at least once a year in all patients, and every three to six months in
patients whose eGFR is below 60 or who are older than 65.

## Dose limits by eGFR

When eGFR falls to between 30 and 45, review the benefit and risk of continuing and cap
the dose at 1,000 mg per day. Stop metformin when eGFR falls below 30.

## Iodinated contrast

Stop metformin at the time of, or before, an iodinated contrast procedure in patients
with eGFR between 30 and 60. Recheck eGFR 48 hours after the procedure and restart
metformin only if renal function is stable.
