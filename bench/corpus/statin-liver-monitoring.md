# Atorvastatin tablets: liver monitoring (bench fixture)

Synthetic bench fixture written for Driftline's load test. It is not medical guidance.

## Indications

Atorvastatin is an HMG-CoA reductase inhibitor used with diet to reduce LDL cholesterol
and the risk of myocardial infarction and stroke in adults with risk factors for
coronary heart disease.

## Liver enzyme monitoring

Check ALT (alanine aminotransferase) before starting therapy. During therapy, check ALT
monthly for the first three months, then every six months, and whenever the patient has
symptoms of liver injury such as fatigue, anorexia, dark urine or jaundice.

## When to stop

Stop atorvastatin if ALT rises above three times the upper limit of normal on two
consecutive measurements, or if the patient develops clinical signs of liver injury
with hyperbilirubinemia. Do not restart without specialist review.

## Muscle effects

Myopathy and rhabdomyolysis have been reported. Ask patients to report unexplained
muscle pain, tenderness or weakness, particularly with fever or malaise. Check creatine
kinase when muscle symptoms appear.

## Dosing

The usual starting dose is 10 mg or 20 mg once daily. The dose range is 10 mg to 80 mg
once daily, taken at any time of day with or without food. Reassess lipid levels four
weeks after starting or changing the dose.
