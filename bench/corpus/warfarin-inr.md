# Warfarin: INR monitoring and interactions (bench fixture)

Synthetic bench fixture written for Driftline's load test. It is not medical guidance.

## INR targets

For most indications, including atrial fibrillation and venous thromboembolism, the
target INR is 2.5 with an acceptable range of 2.0 to 3.0. Mechanical mitral valves need
a target of 3.0, range 2.5 to 3.5.

## How often to check INR

Check INR daily or every other day until it is in range on two consecutive tests, then
twice weekly for two weeks. Once stable, check INR every four weeks. Check again within
one week of any change in dose or interacting medicine.

## Interactions

Antibiotics such as metronidazole and fluconazole raise INR. Rifampicin and
carbamazepine lower it. Avoid starting NSAIDs during warfarin therapy because they raise
the bleeding risk without changing INR.

## Bleeding

For an INR above 9 without bleeding, withhold warfarin and give oral vitamin K. For major
bleeding at any INR, give intravenous vitamin K and prothrombin complex concentrate.
